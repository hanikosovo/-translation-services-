Terminology matters

A pharmacist's termbase, not Google Translate.

Consistent terminology is what separates a leaflet a regulator accepts from one that gets sent back. A sample from the glossary I work from:

English termArabic — العربيةNote
Active pharmaceutical ingredientالمادة الفعالة الدوائيةNot a literal “active element” — the regulatory term.
Dosage regimenنظام الجرعاتDose + frequency + duration, as one concept.
Contraindicationموانع الاستعمالPlural form used on Syrian & MENA leaflets.
Adverse drug reactionالتفاعل الدوائي الضارKept distinct from “side effect” (أثر جانبي).
Patient Information Leafletنشرة معلومات المريضPlain-language register, short sentences.
Shelf life / Expiryمدة الصلاحية / تاريخ الانتهاءTwo different concepts — never merged.

Questions

Before you ask

Do you use machine translation?

No raw machine output is ever delivered. I translate as a human, use my own pharma termbase for consistency, and proofread every job as a separate second pass. That's the whole point of hiring a pharmacist.

How do I count my words?

In Word: Review → Word Count. Or skip it — send the file with your request and I'll count and quote it for free. A normal page is roughly 250 words.

How do I pay from outside Syria / how do I get paid issues?

Payment method is agreed in writing before work starts, based on what works in your country — I'll lay out the options in my quote reply so there are no surprises for either side.

Is my document confidential?

Yes. Client files are never published, shared, or used as samples. Published samples on request are anonymised excerpts only, shared privately with your quote.

Can you do a certified / sworn translation?

I provide accurate professional translation. If you need a sworn stamp for a court or ministry, tell me in your request — I'll tell you honestly whether your document needs a licensed sworn translator instead.
